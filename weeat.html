Overview

Finding food with food restrictions—especially as a group—is hard! Our team at WeEat is tackling this problem today by creating an easy to use app that allows users to find and vote for places to eat as a group. Our product features personalized recommendations, food boards, and an AI helper to support you and your group along the way.

Research

Theme 1: Feeling like a burden to friends

  • participants experienced feelings of awkwardness and frustration when communicating with friends

Theme 2: Difficulty coordinating with friends

  • going out to eat with friends who don't share the same dietary restrictions was a bit more difficult

Theme 3: Wished for more information from restaurants

  • participants mentioned more information or clarity about dietary restrictions from restaurants

Our Goal

How might we design to help UW students support each other to find accurate information of restaurants that work within their food restrictions for all ethnic groups in the Seattle area?

Initial Concepts

Based on user research, we explored 3 early design directions addressing group dining under dietary constraints:

  • WeEat Board - a collaborative restaurant tagging and sharing platform
  • AI Voting Groupchat - an AI-assisted group chat for preference aggregation and voting
  • WeEat Certification - a physical-digital certification system for dietary-friendly restaurants

Each concept emphasized a different dimension of the problem: community knowledge sharing, AI-supported coordination, and trustworthy restaurant information.

Chosen Idea

After presenting our 3 initial concepts, instructor feedback highlighted the strong potential of the AI-assisted group voting experience - especially its ability to reduce decision-making burden when multiple dietary restrictions are involved.

However, we also recognized that AI alone did not address long-term trust in restaurant information.

As a result, we combined two concepts:

  • The AI Voting Groupchat, which supports real-time group coordination
  • The WeEat Certification system, which provides verified, community-driven restaurant ratings

This hybrid approach → WeEat becomes both intelligently guided & structurally supported
