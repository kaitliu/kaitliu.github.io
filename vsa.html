Graphic Design

Overview

As a graphic design officer for the Vietnamese Student Association (VSA) at the University of Washington, I created designs for the membership card and t-shirt, designed promotional graphics for biweekly general meetings, and developed a branding kit for Phở King, VSA's biennial intercollegiate cultural event. Each of which have increased engagement within the 100+ member community, bringing together Vietnamese and other cultures alike, as well as contributing to fundraising efforts.
